Research Interests

I am broadly interested in the analysis and numerics of partial differential equations that emerge from fluid mechanics. Currently, my research investigates the regularity and stability of solutions of a parameterized family of nonlinear wave equations. Such a family includes some well known equations such as the Hunter-Saxton, Camassa-Holm, and Novikov equations, as well as hyperbolic conservation laws.
