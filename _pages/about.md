I am currently a graduate student in mathematics at the University of Kansas. I am broadly interested in the analysis and numerics of partial differential equations that arise from mathematical physics. Currently, my research investigates the regularity and stability of solutions of a parameterized family of nonlinear wave equations. Such a family includes some well known equations such as the Hunter-Saxton, Camassa-Holm, and Novikov equations.
